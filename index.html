What it does

Four ways to explore the world.

01

Live Earth View

Continuously updated satellite data streams to your globe — showing current cloud cover, precipitation, lightning, storm tracks, ocean temperatures, and the live position of the sun and moon. Your globe is never the same twice.

02

AI Explorer Mode

Ask anything. "Show me where volcanoes erupted this year." "Where did the Silk Road run?" Your globe understands the question and projects a visual answer — no app required, just speak or type from any device.

03

Historical Journeys

Animate human history from the first migrations out of Africa to the fall of Rome to moon landings. Watch empires rise and fall, trade routes bloom, and exploration unfold — frame by frame, in the span of a minute.

04

Your World

Mark every country you've visited, every city that matters to you. Share a map with someone you love. Build a custom overlay for your work, your research, or just your favorite baseball teams. It's your globe.
